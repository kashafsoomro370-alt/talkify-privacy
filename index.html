Talkify Privacy Policy

App: Talkify (Android) · Effective date: October 2026 · Contact: talkifyelocution@gmail.com

The short version
  • Talkify uses your microphone only during a speaking round, to measure when you are talking and when you pause.
  • Your voice is analysed on your phone as you speak. It is never recorded to a file, never stored and never sent anywhere, and it is never shared with Google or any ad company.
  • There is no account and no sign-up. Your goal, birth year, practice results, streak and XP are stored only on your phone. We cannot see them.
  • Talkify shows ads provided by Google AdMob. When ads are shown, Google’s ads software in the app collects some device and usage information (such as your IP address, app interactions, diagnostics and your advertising ID) and shares it with Google for ads, analytics and fraud prevention. See section 5.
  • If the birth year you entered means you may be under 18, you only get non-personalised ads with a maximum ad content rating of PG or stricter.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent message asks adult users before ads are personalised. Anyone can stop personalised ads by deleting their advertising ID in Android Settings.
  • You can delete everything the app stores at any time: Profile → Delete all my data.

1. Who we are

This privacy policy applies to the Talkify Android app (“Talkify”, “the app”, “we”, “us”), a 60-second daily speaking practice app for students, distributed on Google Play. The developer named on Talkify’s Google Play store listing is responsible for the app. For any privacy question or request, email talkifyelocution@gmail.com.

2. Your microphone and voice

Talkify needs the microphone for its speaking rounds. Before Android asks for permission, the app explains why it needs the microphone. You can choose “Not now” and allow it later, or turn it off at any time in your phone’s Settings.

  • When it is used: only while you are on the speaking screen: during the short preparation countdown (so the microphone is ready) and the 60-second round. The microphone is switched off when the round ends, when you close the round, or when you leave the app. Talkify never uses the microphone in the background.
  • What happens to the sound: while you speak, the app measures how loud the sound is and how high or low your voice is (its pitch), about 20 times per second, directly on your phone. From these measurements it works out your talk time, your pauses, how steady your voice energy is and how much your tone varies, and turns them into a speaking score. Only these resulting numbers are saved on your phone. The sound itself is never written to a file, never kept after the round, and never sent off your phone.
  • What it does not do: Talkify does not turn your speech into text, does not use speech-recognition services, and does not analyse who you are, your accent or what you said.
  • Not used for ads: your voice, the sound from the microphone and your practice results are never given to Google AdMob or any other ad company, and are never used to choose ads.

4. What we do and do not collect

We, the developer, do not receive your personal data from the app. Talkify:

  • has no account, login or sign-up, and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or school;
  • does not store or send audio recordings;
  • does not ask for permission to use your location, contacts, photos, camera or files (Google may estimate your general location from your IP address when it shows ads; see section 5);
  • has no servers of its own and contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools of its own.

The only third-party software in Talkify that sends data off your phone is Google’s ads software (the Google Mobile Ads SDK, with Google’s consent tool), described in section 5. That data goes to Google, not to us. We see only summary reports from AdMob (for example, how many ads were shown and what they earned), which do not identify you.

Google Play, which you use to download the app, also processes some information under Google’s own privacy policy. That processing is controlled by Google, not by us.

5. Ads from Google AdMob

Talkify is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob, using Google’s official ads software for Android apps (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). Some early test versions of Talkify may not show ads yet. When ads are shown, the following applies.

What Google’s ads software collects

According to Google, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following information, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device;
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • Diagnostic information about how the app and the ads software perform, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use;
  • Device and account identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device.

Apps that use Google ads also share information such as the name of the app with Google. Google says this data is encrypted in transit (sent using TLS).

How Google uses it

This information is sent directly from your phone to Google. Google uses it to show ads, to measure how well ads work, to protect against fraud and abuse, and to maintain and improve its services. Where ad personalisation is allowed, Google may also use it to personalise the ads you see. Google decides which ads appear and may work with other ad companies to fill ad requests, under Google’s own policies. Google’s use of this data is explained in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy. Talkify does not add any other ad network or advertising tool.

What is never shared with ads

Talkify never passes your voice or microphone sound, your practice results, your goal, your feedback reports or your birth year to Google AdMob or to any other ad company.

Users under 18

If the birth year you entered in Talkify means you are, or could still be, under 18, Talkify:

  • tells Google to treat your ad requests as coming from a user under the age of consent, so personalised ads and remarketing are turned off and you only see non-personalised ads; and
  • sets a maximum ad content rating of PG (parental guidance) or stricter, the same limit Google uses for family apps with a mixed audience.

Non-personalised ads are not based on your interests or past activity, but they can still be based on things like the app you are using and your general location, and Google can still use the information listed above to measure ads and prevent fraud. If Talkify does not have a birth year for you, it uses these under-18 settings.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Talkify uses Google’s consent tool (Google’s User Messaging Platform) to show a consent message before ads are personalised. The message lists the ad partners involved and lets you agree or decline. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they won’t be personalised, and Google may show limited ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from Profile → Privacy choices in the app. Users whom Talkify treats as under 18 may not be shown this message, because personalised ads are already turned off for them.

Rewarded ads are optional

Talkify may offer rewarded ads: a short ad you can choose to watch in exchange for a small in-app reward. Watching one is always your choice. You can say no and keep using the app.

How to stop personalised ads

  • Delete your advertising ID: on your Android phone, open Settings → Privacy → Ads and tap Delete advertising ID (the menu names can differ slightly between phone makers and Android versions). Without an advertising ID, ads can’t be personalised using it. You will still see ads, but they may be less relevant. You can also Reset advertising ID there to get a new one.
  • Google ad settings: if you have a Google account, you can turn off ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: change your choice at any time in Profile → Privacy choices.

6. Feedback reports and emails

After a round you can tap “Report a problem with this feedback”. The report (the reasons you choose and an optional note) is saved only on your phone. Please don’t include your name, school or contact details in the note.

If you then tap “Email it to Talkify”, the app opens your own email app with a draft addressed to talkifyelocution@gmail.com. The draft contains your report, the round’s numbers (topic, talk time, pauses, timing) and the app version. It never contains audio. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app. If you send it, we receive the email together with your email address, as with any email. Likewise, if you use “Contact us” in the app, your email app opens and you decide what to send.

We use emails only to answer you and to improve Talkify. We do not sell them or share them with anyone, and we delete them when they are no longer needed (normally within 12 months), or sooner if you ask.

8. Sharing with others

  • Google (AdMob): when ads are shown, Google’s ads software in the app sends the information listed in section 5 directly to Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This is the only sharing that happens automatically.
  • Emails you choose to send us (section 6). We do not share these with anyone.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose emails you have sent us if the law requires it.

Your voice, microphone sound, practice results, goal, birth year and feedback reports are never shared with anyone unless you choose to email a report to us.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your phone: your information stays until you delete it. Go to Profile → Delete all my data → Delete everything to erase your goal, birth year, streak, XP, every round and every saved report from the app straight away. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings deletes everything the app keeps on your phone, including any consent choice saved by Google’s consent tool.
  • Audio: never kept, so there is nothing to delete.
  • Ad data held by Google: we do not receive or store it, so we can’t delete it ourselves. Google keeps it according to the Google Privacy Policy. You can delete or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings (see section 5) and manage your Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • Emails you sent us: email talkifyelocution@gmail.com and we will delete them.

10. Security

Your Talkify information is kept in the app’s private storage on your phone, which Android protects from other apps, and Talkify does not send it over the internet. Google says the data its ads software collects is encrypted in transit using TLS. Anyone who can unlock your phone and open the app can see your progress, so protect your phone with a screen lock.

11. Children and teens

Talkify is designed for students aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children: everything the app stores stays on the user’s own phone, and we do not receive it. The birth year question has no default answer and doesn’t encourage anyone to give a false age.

Users whose birth year shows they may be under 18 (including anyone under 13 who uses the app anyway) get the safer ad settings described in section 5: non-personalised ads only, with a maximum ad content rating of PG or stricter.

If you are a parent or guardian and your child has emailed us, contact talkifyelocution@gmail.com and we will delete that email. Parents can also delete all app data on the child’s phone through Profile → Delete all my data, and can delete or reset the phone’s advertising ID in Android Settings.

13. Your choices and rights

You are in control: you can refuse or withdraw microphone access in Android Settings, change your goal, delete all app data at any time, delete or reset your advertising ID, turn off ad personalisation in your Google ad settings and, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, change your ad consent in Profile → Privacy choices.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or the UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. The only personal data we, the developer, could hold about you is an email you send us. For data collected by Google’s ads software, the Google Privacy Policy explains how to use your rights with Google. Email talkifyelocution@gmail.com with any request and we will help where we can.

We will not start any new collection or sharing of your data without first updating this policy and, where required, asking for your consent in the app.
